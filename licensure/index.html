How it works

End-to-end licensure and credential monitoring.

  1. Digitally onboard staff

    Bring practitioners and staff into the platform digitally, with the licenses and credentials they hold.

  2. Establish your credentialed providers

    Build a verified roster of credentialed providers for your medical staff.

What does IntegrityShield-Licensure do?

It verifies and continuously monitors the licenses and credentials of your practitioners and staff, checks their status and disciplinary actions, and alerts you before anything lapses.

What is primary source verification (PSV)?

Confirming a license or credential directly with the issuing source, such as a state licensing board. IntegrityShield-Licensure automates these checks and keeps the documentation.

Which countries does it cover?

Broad coverage across primary sources in the USA, Canada and Australia. New sources are added continuously.

Does it check for disciplinary actions?

Yes. Along with license status and expiration, it checks for disciplinary actions and alerts you when one appears.

Who uses it?

Medical Staff Offices, credentialing teams, HR and compliance teams responsible for licensed practitioners and staff.

Will it work with SES and COI?

Yes. All three IntegrityShield solutions share one platform, so a practitioner can be screened for exclusions, conflicts of interest and licensure in one place.
