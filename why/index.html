Why IntegrityShield

Every name on your roster is a promise.

That the clinician at the bedside is licensed. That the vendor you pay is allowed to bill federal programs. That no one is quietly being paid to steer a decision. Keeping that promise every day is harder than it looks.

I The reality

One person. More than a hundred places to look.

A single physician, nurse or vendor can appear on federal exclusion lists, on any of 47 state Medicaid lists, with a licensing board in every state they practice in, and in the federal record of industry payments.

Every source keeps its own calendar.

OIG updates its exclusion list monthly. States publish when they publish. A board changes a license the day it acts. None of them tells you when something changes.

And every answer expires.

A clean result is true on the day you look. By the next morning, it is a memory of that day.

II The patchwork

Most integrity programs were assembled one requirement at a time.

Exclusions with one vendor. Licenses in a spreadsheet. Conflicts of interest on a disclosure form, signed once a year. Each piece made sense on the day it was added.

Together they leave one question that nobody can answer quickly: what has changed since the last time anyone looked?

III Between the checks

A check is a photograph.

Most programs take one a month. Each is accurate for the moment it was taken.

Life happens in between.

An exclusion posted on the 7th waits for the next check. So does one posted on the 20th. In the meantime, that person keeps working and that vendor keeps billing.

The risk lives in the gaps.

Federal health programs will not pay for care furnished by an excluded person, and those payments can be recovered. The weeks between checks are where the exposure adds up.

Now look every night.

Screen every night instead of every month, and each of those gaps shrinks to a single day. What would have waited weeks is in front of you by morning.

IV A better way to think about it

Integrity is not a checkpoint you pass once. It is a condition you keep, and can prove, every day.

  1. 01Look everywhere it matters.

    Every federal and state source that applies to the people and companies you work with, not just the two lists everyone remembers.

  2. 02Look again, all the time.

    Every night for exclusions, every week for licenses, and the moment a record is added or changed.

  3. 03Show only what needs a decision.

    Match on identity, not just on names, and keep the evidence of every decision you make.

V IntegrityShield

One record. Everything you need to know about it.

IntegrityShield brings exclusions, conflicts of interest and licensure together on one platform, for every employee, contractor, vendor and practitioner you work with.

Exclusions, every night.

IntegrityShield-HSES checks your records against 100+ federal and state sources each night, and screens new records the moment they arrive. Matching on NPI, date of birth and address clears name collisions, so only possible matches reach your team.

Relationships, made visible.

IntegrityShield-COI matches your vendors and practitioners against CMS Open Payments, shows every reported payment by amount, year and type, and works suspect cases from open to closed.

Licenses, checked at the source.

IntegrityShield-LCM verifies licenses with 80+ licensing boards and regulators in the USA, Canada and Australia, rechecks them every week, and reminds you before anything lapses.
