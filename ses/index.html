How it works

Live in under an hour.

  1. Load your workforce, vendors and providers

    Import employees, contractors, vendors and healthcare providers, or connect systems such as Workday and MD-Staff.

  2. Nightly automated screenings

    Each record is checked against OIG LEIE, SAM.gov and state Medicaid exclusion lists, and again whenever a list changes.

  3. Review only matches that need attention

    Identity matching on NPI, date of birth and address clears false positives, so you only see the possible matches that matter.

  4. Manage true positives with configurable workflows

    Route each confirmed match to the right person, track it to closure and keep every decision for your next audit.

Data sources and limitations

What’s covered, and what isn’t.

Covered

  • Federal lists: OIG LEIE, SAM.gov exclusions, the FDA debarment list and the national sex offender registry
  • 47 state Medicaid exclusion lists from 43 states and DC, plus state abuse registries and nurse imposter lists
  • Identity data from sources such as NPPES and CMS, used to confirm matches

Limitations

  • A name match is a possible match, not a confirmed identity: matches need review before you act.
  • Results are only as current and complete as the lists their publishers release.
  • Lists that are not in the source list on this page are not screened.

Sanctions & exclusion screening FAQs.

Or call 1-800-604-1354.

What is sanctions and exclusion screening software?

Software that checks employees, vendors and healthcare providers against government sanctions and exclusion lists, such as OIG LEIE, SAM.gov and state Medicaid exclusion lists, and flags matches for review. IntegrityShield-HSES does this every night, in-house.

Is IntegrityShield-HSES an all-in-one tool for employees, vendors and healthcare providers?

Yes. One platform screens your workforce, contractors, vendors and healthcare providers against federal and state lists, including OIG LEIE, SAM.gov exclusions and state Medicaid exclusion lists.

Which official exclusion lists does it check?

OIG LEIE, SAM.gov exclusions, the FDA debarment list, and state Medicaid exclusion and termination lists from official government sources, refreshed every night.

What should sanctions screening software include in 2026?

Coverage of OIG, SAM and every state Medicaid list you are subject to; nightly rescreening; match scoring that clears false positives; an audit trail; and integrations with HR, credentialing and vendor systems. IntegrityShield-HSES includes each of these.

How quickly can we start?

Most teams are live in under an hour from activation, not counting integrations.

Can we stop outsourcing?

Yes. Run screening in-house for up to 90% less than outsourcing, based on our internal cost calculations, with an audit trail reviewers can follow.

Does it connect to our systems?

Yes. Integrations include Workday and MD-Staff, and APIs connect to HR, credentialing, contract management and billing systems.

How does IntegrityShield-HSES reduce false positives?

Identity matching compares names with NPI, date of birth and address, using identity data from sources such as NPPES and CMS, and clears 90% of false positives, so your team only reviews identity matches that need attention.

What happens when a true match is found?

You get an instant alert, and the match is routed through a configurable workflow to the right reviewer. Every step and decision is logged for your audit trail.

Who should be screened?

Employees, contractors, healthcare providers, vendors, board members and anyone else your compliance program requires you to monitor.

How often should we screen against the OIG exclusion list?

OIG expects regular checks, and many organizations screen monthly. IntegrityShield-HSES screens every night and again whenever a list updates, so nothing waits for the next monthly run.

Is there a free option?

Yes. You can run a free integrity violation screening check on this page, and the Free plan covers up to 25 active monitoring records.

What happens when a list updates or we add a record?

Screening runs both ways, automatically. When a sanctions or exclusion source updates, your whole watchlist is rescreened, and when you add or change a record, that record is screened right away.

Can HR screen a candidate before hiring?

Yes. Run an ad hoc check on a prospective employee in-house, in real time, so hiring is not held up waiting for an outside vendor.

Can it check medical bills before they go to payers?

Yes. With a medical billing system integration, IntegrityShield-HSES checks the providers on a superbill in real time before it is submitted, which helps you avoid billing for excluded providers and the OIG audits and penalties that follow.

Can we see match results in our own systems?

Yes. Connect your contract management system to see match results there, and connect your medical staff credentialing and privileging system for real-time checks on every medical service provider.

What dashboards and reports are included?

Management dashboards and reporting templates show your screening activity, open matches and their resolution at a glance, ready to share with leadership or auditors.

How do we get started?

Register and sign up, add your watchlist records, set up real-time alerts and notifications if you want them, and review screening matches from your dashboard.
