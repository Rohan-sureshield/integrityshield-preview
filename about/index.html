About IntegrityShieldThe integrity side of SureShield.

IntegrityShield is the healthcare integrity platform from SureShield, Inc., an Atlanta software company that builds automated security, compliance and integrity tools. It exists so healthcare organizations can know, and show, who they employ, contract with and pay.

Meet our team Contact us
Why IntegrityShield exists

Healthcare integrity work was still being done by hand.

SureShield started from a plain observation: in most organizations, security, compliance and integrity work is complicated, disjointed and expensive. Its leaders had spent two decades building compliance, safety and data analytics companies, and they set out to make that work continuous and automated instead.

Healthcare made the integrity part impossible to ignore. Every hospital, practice and health plan has to show that the people and companies it works with are not excluded from federal programs, are licensed to practice, and have no undisclosed financial ties. In most places that proof was still pulled together from separate lists, separate vendors and separate spreadsheets.

IntegrityShield is SureShield’s answer to that work: the same automated, resource-lite approach, focused entirely on healthcare integrity.

What we stand for

SureShield’s six principles, applied to integrity.

  1. 01Safety

    Integrity screening is patient safety. The people and companies around a patient should be the ones they are supposed to be.

  2. 02Simplicity

    A compliance officer should not need an IT project to know who they work with. Most teams are live in under an hour.

  3. 03Value

    Doing the right thing should not cost more than outsourcing it. Running it in-house should cost less.

  4. 04Integrated automation

    The checks, the rechecks and the alerts should run on their own, and in the systems you already have.

  5. 05Actionable insight

    A result is only useful if it says what to do next, and leaves a record that someone did it.

  6. 06Customer success

    We measure ourselves by how easy our customers’ audits become.

Who makes IntegrityShield?

SureShield, Inc., a security, compliance and integrity (SCI) software company based in Atlanta, Georgia. IntegrityShield is its integrity platform for healthcare.

What does IntegrityShield do?

It screens employees, vendors and healthcare providers against OIG, SAM and 100+ federal and state exclusion sources every night, and monitors conflicts of interest between vendors and credentialed practitioners using CMS Open Payments.

Who is IntegrityShield for?

Compliance, credentialing and medical staff teams at hospitals and health systems, physician groups, post-acute and behavioral health providers, health plans, pharmacies, life sciences companies and other organizations that work with federal healthcare programs.
